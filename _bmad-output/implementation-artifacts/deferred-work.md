## Deferred from: 4-digit PIN change (2026-09-06)

- **No rate limiting or lockout on `POST /api/auth/login`** [`src/app/api/auth/login/route.ts`] — There is no attempt throttling, no per-device backoff, and no account lockout. Fixing PIN length at 4 (PRD FR38, amended 2026-09-06) reduces the keyspace to 10,000 combinations, so an unattended tablet on the restaurant LAN can be walked through the entire space. The existing constant-time loop over all staff protects against timing oracles but does nothing about volume. **This became materially more important with the 4-digit change and should be closed before the system handles real money.** Options: per-IP/per-device exponential backoff, a short lockout after N failures, or a delay that grows with recent failure count. Belongs with Epic 2's auth work or a dedicated hardening story.
- **Existing 5- and 6-digit PINs would be unenterable** [`src/server/db/seed.ts`, staff records] — The login schema now rejects anything but 4 digits, and the pad cannot enter a fifth. All seeded PINs are 4 digits so nothing is affected today, but any real deployment that already issued a longer PIN would lock that staff member out with "PIN must be 4 digits". Needs a migration or a re-issue step before this ships to a site with existing accounts. No such site exists yet.

- **Three chip rows now exist with no shared abstraction** [`zone-chip-bar.tsx`, `menu-category-chips.tsx`, `seat-chip-row.tsx`] - Each is a horizontally scrollable row of `h-touch-waiter` pills with an active state, and each has its own copy of the class list.
